A force is a push or a pull that can change the speed, direction or shape of an object. Newton's first law says that an object stays at rest, or keeps moving in a straight line at constant speed, unless an unbalanced force acts on it. This property of objects is called inertia. Newton's second law says that the acceleration of an object depends on the force applied and on its mass: the same force produces a smaller acceleration in a heavier object. Newton's third law says that when one object pushes on another, the second object pushes back with an equal force in the opposite direction. Friction is a force that opposes motion between surfaces that touch each other, and it slows moving objects down. Force is measured in newtons.
